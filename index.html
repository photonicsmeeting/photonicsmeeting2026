About

OSM Photonics Meeting 2026

The OSM Photonics Meeting 2026 (PM26) is an annual event organised by the Optical Society of Malaysia (OSM) in celebrating International Day of Light on 16th of May every year.

In this PM26, program has been arranged to attract researchers, especially in photonics and optics. Program such as conference and webinar will fill this two-days event.

The primary goal of PM26 is to allow researchers and scientists to share advances in optical engineering and applications, nanotechnology, while exchanging scientific knowledge among participants.

This program is a high impact program which was constructed to build a collaboration and joint effort with others institutions especially in the field of Optic and Photonics research. The purpose of this program is also to gather experts in Lasers, Optics and Photonics field to contribute in delivering ideas and experiences to all participants involved.

Manuscript Submission and Review

All submitted manuscripts will undergo a double-blind peer review process. To ensure anonymity during the review, authors must remove all identifying information from their manuscripts prior to submission. This includes author names, institutional affiliations, email addresses, and the Acknowledgement section. Authors should also ensure that any self-references within the manuscript do not reveal their identity. Manuscripts that do not comply with the double-blind review requirements may be returned for revision before being sent for peer review.

Only original research papers are accepted for submission. Review papers, survey papers, literature reviews, and previously published or simultaneously submitted manuscripts will not be considered for publication. Authors are expected to submit original, unpublished work that presents novel research findings and significant contributions to the fields of optics, photonics, and related technologies.
